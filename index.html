The God of Abraham

I Was Born

My mom believes the Bible but doesn't believe her eyes and thinks I'm schizophrenic, but I can demonstrate how Gnosticism inverts true Biblical Christianity. I started studying alchemy when I went to college because my dad was big into the Jesus thing and I needed to find the truth for myself. When I realized that this work has already been finished through Jesus Christ, it was shocking. It reveals that Jesus is who he said he was, and now my life is different.

My dad is a fundamentalist Christian and I have a very different belief and view of the world. I have an open mind, and instead of blinding myself to close minded religious dogma I decided to actually do some research into my beliefs. My beliefs are based on facts instead of make believe fairy tales and it confuses me why nobody listens to what I have to say.

My Mom Abused Me

My mom is a Psychopath, Narcissist, and a Sociopath. She lied to my dad about who she was when they were dating and had every intention of fucking his life up once they got married. As her son, I've dealt with emotional neglect and I watched the power struggle my dad had in trying to control her temper tantrums while growing up. She lied to her family about my mental health as I was experiencing a spiritual awakening, and as a result I've had to deal with abuse from other family members as well. I am not who she says I am. It's the world that is crazy, not me.

Based on the lore of The Binding of Isaac, the relationship between Isaac and his mother (Magdalene) is depicted as intensely strained and, in his imagination, traumatic, stemming from extreme religious zealotry and severe mental illness.

I Went To School

While in Highschool I had a crush on a girl named Annie. I was sitting next to her in a class on mythology and got to see her every day. It was the first class of the day and I'm a sleepy boi so I slept in class. Whatever. I overheard from some friends that she was sexually promiscuous so I was nervous approaching her. I also heard that her dad was abusive, so that scared me even more. Anyway, I moved to college, met an orphan, and never talked to her again.

Meeting My Girlfriend

Shortly after I found God I decided to try online dating from Christian Mingle. I was talking to this girl, Rebecca, while my family was falling apart. I never got around to meeting her and things ended poorly between us because I was going through emotional problems at the time. I find it interesting because Isaac's wife was named Rebecca.

I'm Gonna Be A Dad

Because of my dad's Biblical Fundamentalism and my occult research, I feel as if it is impossible to develop intimate relationships. I am traumatized and forever a preteen stuck in an adult body. I legitimately feel as if my dad watched Jane die and there's nothing I can do about fixing my life situation. In addition to that, because of my mom's defiant attitude, I learned from a very young age not to listen to my dad, so I never learned critical life skills needed in adult life.

We're Having Twins!

This is me and my sister, Kayla. I haven't talked to her since I left my mom, and my mom manipulated our family to include lots of sibling rivalry as Kayla is the "Golden" child while I am the Scapegoat. Anyway, my sister had a friend, Nikki, who's parents got divorced. I found her Twitter account a while ago and she's an artist which I think is cool. One of her tweets involves paperwork from her dad, and I'm not assuming her dad is abusive or anything, but I connect that to whatever happened between Annie and I. Also, there was another girl named Annie who had the same last name as Annie and I'm rather confused about it all. She's married now and changed her last name.

Shorty after I accepted Jesus Christ as my Lord and Savior I joined the college campus Christian group, Intervarsity. I met a girl named Sarah, and we connected, but I saw her more as a sister. I actually liked her friend but I was too shy to say anything about it. I find it interesting because Abraham's wife was named Sarah, and he claimed she was his sister out of fear that local rulers would kill him to take her to be their own.

Let Both Grow Together

So this actually happened. It's my life story, not some made up shit that I pulled out of my ass, but for whatever reason my mom still thinks my dad and I are delusional. I grew up with these people and I've been isolated from everyone because of how people react to Jesus. My mom believes in Jesus but her reaction to me finding Jesus was so repulsive. Everyone's reaction to actual truth and not fairy tale Santa Claus is so absurd that I honestly think there's something fundamentally wrong with human beings.

I Just Want To Be Loved

The way I talk to women is messed up because I desire emotional support for something that deeply troubles me. Nobody says anything to me and I desperately need therapy but I can't explain this to a therapist and expect them to understand. I'm stuck wondering why this happened to me without an explanation. I feel as if my reality is shattered, and due to the personal aspect of everything, I feel as if I'm the only one who knows just how deep this goes.

We're Getting Married!

I'll likely never get married and I'm comfortably numb with how boring and peaceful my life is, I don't need physical intimacy. Although, I do get jealous at times that basically everyone else in my family isn't completely cut off from the rest of the world. Aslo, like my lack of real world skills prevents me from even desiring a relationship, because I don't need the added responsibility.

Surprisingly, this all happened in Michigan. The orphan I knew had her parents die in a motorcycle accident on Marshall Road, and Marshall Mathers is famously from Detroit, Michigan. Apparently Satan exists, I never would've believed it if I didn't literally see it. It's really fucked up that this isn't public knowledge. The concept of Hell is really fucked up.

I Started A New Job

I haven't worked in over 10 years because I get income from Social Security for a disability I don't have. Basically everyone on the planet incorrectly assumes I'm mentally ill so I get free money from the state, which is nice. My dad's devotion to actually following the Bible blesses us with this opportunity to just do nothing, and I like it.

I Finally Got To Retire

Due to being on Social Security, I basically got to retire while I was in my mid 20's. I don't do anything with my life besides loaf around, occasionally I have fun with my dad on the weekends. I have no friends, so I don't know what else to do. I'm too depressed to watch TV so I shitpost on the internet.

I Died

What's this life for anyway? We work until we die, and if we're lucky enough to retire, we're too old to be spontaneous and adventurous. I took the left hand path in my early 20's and now I'm a vegetable, waiting to die, because the world is stuck in a delusion.
